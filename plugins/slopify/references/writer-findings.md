# Spike plan and findings

## Plan

List the claims the question depends on, each with its evidence status (supported, contested, or unknown) from the supplied evidence, and for each claim that needs a measurement, the measurement that would settle it.

## Findings document

Write the entry where it best fits the project's existing documentation, following its conventions. The first line under its title is `Spike: <slug>`. Then give:

- the question, verbatim;
- the findings;
- the measurements, each with its command and value;
- the open unknowns, including claims no evidence settled.

Every claim cites its source: a `path:line`, library evidence, an outside source, or a prototype measurement. A claim without one is an unknown, not a finding. Make no recommendation: report what the evidence shows, and leave the choice to the reader.
