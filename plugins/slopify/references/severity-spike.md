# Severity of a spike's findings

A findings document answers a bounded question from cited evidence and measurements, and recommends nothing. Rate a problem in it by what it is.

- **blocker:** the document doesn't answer its question, or a measurement the answer rests on didn't run.
- **major:** any of these:
  - a claim with no cited source or measurement behind it, or one its source doesn't support;
  - a measurement that doesn't reproduce from the command the document gives;
  - a recommendation, or a choice presented as the answer;
  - an unknown the evidence leaves open that the document doesn't name;
  - a citation that is broken or points to the wrong passage.

Anything real that is neither is minor or a nit, as the brief defines them.
