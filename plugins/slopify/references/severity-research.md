# Severity of a library change

A research change exists to say only what its sources support. Rate a problem in its wiki pages and raw notes by what it is.

- **blocker:** the pages don't answer the question, or the answer rests on no evidence the library holds.
- **major:** any of these, on any line the change wrote:
  - a claim that the block it cites doesn't support, or that cites nothing when it needs evidence;
  - a claim credited to a source, author, or section that doesn't say it;
  - a claim that contradicts its own sources, or another claim on the same page;
  - a result stated more broadly than its source reports it, such as one benchmark's result stated for two;
  - a citation that is broken or ambiguous, such as a block ID defined twice in one raw note.

Anything real that is neither is minor or a nit, as the brief defines them: wording, ordering, or a missing nuance that leaves every claim true.
